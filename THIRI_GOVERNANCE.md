- **Pronunciation & Voice Synthesis (TTS)**:
  - When voicing or synthesizing **Thiri** / **THIRI**, it must always sound like **"theory"** (the music theory word: /ˈθɪəri/ or /ˈθiː.ə.ɹi/).
  - For TTS pipelines (ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Google TTS, Whisper, etc.), if the raw spelling "Thiri" produces an incorrect pronunciation (such as "thee-ree" or "thigh-ree"), pass the phonetic spelling or replacement `Theory` to the speech generator while keeping the written transcripts, captions, and display text as `THIRI`.

## Harmonic Rules & Voicing Architecture

Every musical note generated by the station is governed by strict mathematical music theory algorithms implemented in `live_harmonic_generator.py`:

### 1. Mid-Register Voicing: THIRI Drop-2
- **Register**: C3 (130.8 Hz) to G4 (392.0 Hz).
- **Algorithm**: The second note from the top of the closed four-way voicing is dropped down exactly one octave.
- **Role**: Provides warm, open harmonic foundation without mud in the lower-mid range.

### 2. Upper-Register Voicing: THIRI Drop-3
- **Register**: C4 (261.6 Hz) to C6 (1046.5 Hz).
- **Algorithm**: The third note from the top of the chord voicing is dropped down an octave, leaving the top melody note isolated with acoustic air.
- **Role**: Adds delicate sparkle and contrapuntal separation panned right opposite the Rhodes.

### 3. Guide Tone Mandatory Presence
- Every chord voicing must explicitly include the **3rd** and the **7th** scale degree (or 6th in 6/9 chords).
- Guide tones establish the harmonic identity (major, minor, dominant, half-diminished) and provide smooth chromatic voice leading between chord changes.

### 4. Continuous Clam Rate Auditing
- Prior to rendering any 4-bar chunk, `audit_thiri_governance()` evaluates all active note pitches against the target chord scale degree set.
- Non-chord tones with harsh dissonant intervals (unprepared minor 2nds against chord tones) are strictly forbidden:
  - **Guide Tone Compliance**: 100.0%
  - **Clam Rate**: 0.0%
- These metrics are passed directly to the telemetry HUD and displayed on the broadcast stream.
